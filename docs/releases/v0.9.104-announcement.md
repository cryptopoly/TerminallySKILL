# v0.9.104 announcement (draft for Dan to post)

TerminallySKILL 0.9.104 is out.

The headline: it now tells you when a coding agent is waiting for you. When Claude Code, Codex or any other program stops at an approval prompt, the tab turns yellow, says "needs you" and sends a desktop notification. If you are away from the keyboard, reminders follow at 10 and 30 minutes.

Also new:
- Clearer tab dots: running, needs you, finished, failed and idle each look different, with a legend in Help.
- Maximise the terminal with Cmd+Shift+M (Ctrl+Shift+M on Windows and Linux).
- Cycle projects from the keyboard.
- Keep notifications on screen until you close them.
- Ctrl+R shell history search now works from the editor prompt.

Still no telemetry, no analytics and no account.

Download for macOS, Windows and Linux: https://terminallyskill.com
Release notes: https://github.com/cryptopoly/TerminallySKILL/releases/tag/v0.9.104
